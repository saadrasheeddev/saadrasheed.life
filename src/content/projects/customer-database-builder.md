## Problem
Dozens of missed calls daily resulting in zero caller capture, no CRM data, and manual SMS follow-up.

## Solution & Flow
A 24/7 AI phone system that answers inbound calls, logs every customer into your CRM, and builds a database ready for future marketing campaigns. n8n writes the data to Vtiger CRM, triggers a follow-up text, and logs a call summary into a dedicated Telegram channel where the team can reply.

## Tech Stack
VAPI, n8n, Vtiger CRM, Telegram
