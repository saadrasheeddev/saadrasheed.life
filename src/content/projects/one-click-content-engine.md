## Problem
Content creators wasted hours manually formatting video scripts, voiceovers, and YouTube descriptions.

## Solution & Flow
Enter a topic in Google Sheets and instantly generate a full video script, aligned voiceover, and YouTube description in your Drive. Apps Script triggers an LLM to generate the documents in parallel, saving all formatted documents directly to Google Drive.

## Tech Stack
Google Sheets, Apps Script, LLM, Google Drive
