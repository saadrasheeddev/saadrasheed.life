## Problem
Businesses signed NDAs blindly because professional legal review was too slow and expensive.

## Solution & Flow
Upload any NDA to surface missing clauses, ambiguous language, and risky terms. Suggested rewrites are inserted as Word comments. Apps Script extracts the text from Google Forms, an LLM identifies gaps, and a revised Word document is returned with tracked comments.

## Tech Stack
Google Forms, Apps Script, LLM, Word API
