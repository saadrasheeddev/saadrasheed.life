## Problem
Interested visitors browsed the portfolio but bounced before booking a Calendly slot, leading to lost revenue.

## Solution & Flow
Capture visitor emails before they bounce, detect incomplete bookings, and trigger a personalized follow-up sequence automatically. A pop-up captures email, Zapier checks Calendly, and System.io automatically sends a sequence to recover them.

## Tech Stack
Zapier, System.io, Calendly
